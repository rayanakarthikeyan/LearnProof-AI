## Devpost description scaffold

**Problem:** Course knowledge is scattered across videos, slides and textbooks. Generic answers rarely identify why a learner is stuck or provide inspectable course evidence.

**Solution:** LearnProof combines cited multimodal tutoring with diagnostic follow-ups, prerequisite remediation and unseen transfer questions. Its learner model uses scored observations to choose the next step.

**How it works:** Explain the actual ingestion/retrieval implementation, evidence viewer, item verification and BKT event updates. Replace planned details with what was built.
